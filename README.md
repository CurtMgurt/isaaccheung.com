# Curtis pixel room preview

Temporary preview for isaaccheung.com. The Vincent site remains separate.

Open index.html through a local HTTP server. Both machines link to their player sites. The scene uses a 1536×1024 plate, a transparent cat pose atlas, one flipper sprite, and a stylized pixel family print in a fitted landscape frame.

The room has plaster walls, painted wood trim, a recessed sash window and wood flooring. The preserved bridge artwork and its blinking lights are resampled into the glass apertures. A simple striped foreground rug, chair and sofa replace the foreground plants. The hobby desk has a cutting mat, nippers, a knife and tweezers beside one model. Calvin’s right strap forms one straight continuous band.

The canvas runs at 30 fps and 768×512 pixels. Native painted lamps have cached dim and bright variants; the ball and flippers share a timed path. Coin buttons stay illuminated. VINCENT lettering has only a subtle unified variation, and the marquee trim changes slowly. Steam is filled vapor rather than strokes. Pausing freezes all motion together; reduced-motion settings start with a still scene. Hidden or offscreen scenes suspend animation.

Assets and prompt provenance are recorded in the local authoring workspace at mockups/curtis-arcade/lofi-v8/asset-notes.json. Generation drafts and QA images remain outside the published files.
